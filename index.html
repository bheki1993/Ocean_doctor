About Ocean Doctor

Ocean Doctor (PTY) Ltd is the first Telemedicine provider tailored for the fishing and maritime industry in Southern Africa. Our SAHPRA-approved technology ensures 24/7 access to qualified doctors, delivering seamless healthcare to crews at sea, enhancing safety and operational efficiency.

Dr Sage Gerdharee — Medical Director

After graduating Cum Laude from Stellenbosch University Medical School, Dr Sage Gerdharee completed his Medical Internship at Cape Town's Groote Schuur Hospital complex and Community Service in deep rural Transkei. As an Independent Medical Practitioner, he spent much of his time working in cross-country emergency air transfer services, as well as in the private healthcare sector in and around the Cape. Throughout his medical career he’s worked as a freelance digital marketer for South African e-commerce companies and start-ups, growing ideas, businesses and solving problems. With a love for people and technology, building and optimizing healthcare processes has become a niche passion for him.

Bhekumuzi Sodinga — Project Manager

Bhekumuzi brings a strong background in technology, data, and project coordination to Ocean Doctor. With experience in cloud engineering, data analytics, and software development, he combines technical expertise with organizational skills to ensure projects run smoothly and deliver impact. As Project Manager, he oversees planning, execution, and team collaboration, making sure every initiative aligns with Ocean Doctor’s mission to provide reliable telemedicine solutions for the South African fishing industry.

Loyiso Phantshwa — Director

Loyiso studied Law at Rhodes University in the Eastern Cape before beginning his career at a law firm in Cape Town. With a strong foundation in legal and corporate governance, he has since expanded his leadership into the fishing industry. He currently serves as Chairman of Fish SA and as a Director at Ocean Doctor, where he brings strategic vision and industry expertise to support the company’s growth and impact.

Our Services

Through innovative, SAHPRA-approved Telemedicine devices, we provide comprehensive medical access and care to vessels in offshore environments, ensuring crew health and operational success. We supply the Doc on Deck (DOD) kit a rugged telemedicine hardware and an encrypted web app for live consultations, vitals capture and patient benching.

Doc on Deck Kit
SAHPRA-approved Telemedicine hardware kit built for maritime use. It contains Digital Stethoscope, Otoscope, Dermascope, Vitals monitor and Tablet with Telemedicine Software.
DOD Kit with Equipment
Includes vitals + ECG, Glucometer and peripherals for reliable remote diagnosis and follow-up.
Encrypted Web-App
24/7 consultations (phone/whatsapp/video), secure patient records, delayed consult uploads and crew benching.

Our Impact

Enhanced Crew Satisfaction

Crew members and their families have peace of mind when going to sea, feeling protected, cared for, and reassured by their employers.

Reduced Medical Evacuations

We minimize unplanned returns to shore by addressing health concerns early, saving costs on fuel and operations while ensuring a smoother voyage.

Empowered Captains

Captains are relieved of the burden of judging the severity of medical issues alone, with access to expert medical support to make informed decisions.
