Who We Are

Our Purpose

Mission & Why It Matters

Our Mission

Caring for Your Crew, Caring for Your Company

To deliver quality medical access and medical care to vessels in remote offshore environments — giving vessel owners, crew and their families peace of mind when out at sea.

A healthy crew means optimised productivity at sea. Less downtime, less costly diversions due to healthcare issues, and more fish caught.

Optimised Productivity

Healthy crew means fewer interruptions, less unplanned downtime, and more fish caught per voyage.

Reduced Costly Diversions

Early intervention prevents expensive returns to shore — saving fuel, time, and catch value for operators.

Worker Rights & ESG

Supports workers’ right to quality healthcare while strengthening Social and Governance standards. Early medical intervention prevents unnecessary vessel diversions, reducing fuel use and emissions, and improving Environmental impact and overall ESG compliance.

Fully Tax Deductible

Ocean Doctor is fully tax deductible as an employee cost — a smart business and healthcare investment.

Our Healthcare Solution

The DOD Kit & Services

Through innovative, SAHPRA-approved telemedicine technology, we provide comprehensive medical access to vessels in offshore environments — ensuring crew health, captain confidence, and operational success.

SAHPRA-Approved Hardware

The DOD Kit — Doc on Deck

A purpose-built, rugged telemedicine hardware kit designed for the marine environment. Everything a doctor needs to assess and treat your crew remotely — in one weatherproof case.

Vitals Suite: Pulse, temperature, oxygen saturation, blood pressure & 3-lead ECG
Diagnostic Tools: Glucometer, digital otoscope, dermascope & stethoscope
Microsoft Tablet: Encrypted telemedicine software for live video consultations
Field Ready: USB hub, power bank & rugged protective carry case
Encrypted Platform

Encrypted Telemedicine Web App

Our secure, purpose-built digital platform powers every consultation. It supports live video, delayed consult uploads, on-deck patient monitoring, secure crew health records, and reporting for vessel owners.

Live & Scheduled: 24/7 medical consultations — phone, WhatsApp, or video
On-Deck Monitoring: Remote patient monitoring for unwell crew members at sea
Full Prescription Service: Prescriptions, pathology, imaging & referral coordination
Owner Reports: Reporting and support for vessel owners and company management
